# Decisions

One line per decision and why.

- 2026-11-01 Big Echo: skip 30分 tables marked 限定 or 学生パスポート — the name becomes "30分", so a conditional price would show as everyone's price.
- 2026-11-01 Big Echo: take the time from a table's first row only for 30分 tables, and only when the row starts with it — elsewhere it was an entry window or a special plan ("受付時間10:00～11:59", "♪昼3時間パック♪").
- 2026-11-01 Big Echo: a page whose 祝前日 sits in both day columns (石橋店), or Sunday in both and Friday in neither (札幌山鼻店 nights), is kept as written — the contradiction is the shop's, and guessing a side would be inventing.
- 2026-11-01 Big Echo: days-in-heading tables are read only for 30分 prices, and only when every column is a drink plan or there is one column — the pack and free-time tables in that layout carry their own conditions (受付終了, 学生のみ, two time ranges) and stay skipped.
- 2026-11-01 Big Echo: shops that list only 会員 prices are shown with them (owner's choice) — nothing is invented; 一般 simply has no price there, like Manekineko's missing types.
- 2026-11-01 Big Echo: "会員 + ¥50" written in words (水戸, 鉾田, 那珂, 水戸駅南口) is not turned into a 一般 price — it would be a calculated number, not one on the page.
